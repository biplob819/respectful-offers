| OWNER D-1 | 2026-11-15 | The primary result is a multi-period **simulator rollout** of every policy on the same evaluation members under common random numbers (CRN). Inverse propensity scoring (IPS), self-normalised IPS (SNIPS) and doubly robust (DR) estimates are computed on period-1 logged decisions and **validated against the simulator's exact oracle value** (owner ruling, Q3) | OPE on simulated logs as the headline (PRD Flow A); rollouts only, no OPE | When the simulator generates both the logs and the truth, OPE can only recover the YAML (C1). Rules R1, R2 and R8 and the 90-day value are sequential, and single-step OPE cannot evaluate a policy whose allowed actions depend on its own past choices (C3). Validating OPE against a known truth turns the weakness into evidence that the estimators work, which is what a real A/B log would need |
| D-2 | 2026-11-15 | The simulator draws a hidden **response segment** per member (sure thing, persuadable, lost cause, sleeping dog) from a mix that depends on real features (risk band × listening-trend band). Actions have an exposure effect and an acceptance effect per segment | The PRD's renewal-probability table per action × risk band | With a table keyed only on risk band, the optimal policy can be read straight from the YAML and the bandit has nothing to learn (C2).
